Who We Are

About Us

A Place to Worship, Grow & Serve

Zion Power Ministries is a Christ-centered ministry committed to spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ and helping people grow in their relationship with God.

Through worship, prayer, fellowship and the Word of God, we desire to create a loving community where everyone can experience God's grace and discover their purpose.

Connect With Us
Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ, lead people into a personal relationship with God, and help every believer grow in faith through worship, prayer, fellowship, and the Word of God.

Our Vision

To build a Christ-centered community where people experience God's love, discover their God-given purpose, grow spiritually, and become a blessing to their families and society.
